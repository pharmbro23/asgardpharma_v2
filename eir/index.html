The problem

Cloud counting apps send your photos out the door.

Many pill-counting apps upload every photo of your counting tray to a remote AI server to do the counting. Pharmacy counters are busy: a vial label, a bag tag, a hard copy or a screen ends up in the frame. With a cloud app, that patient information leaves your pharmacy with the photo, and nobody has to make a mistake for it to happen.

One label in the frame

A patient's name, Rx number or medication caught in the corner of a photo is personal health information, now sitting on someone else's server.

Data leaves Canada

Large AI providers commonly process requests on servers abroad, outside the reach of Canadian privacy protections.

You can't unsend a photo

Once it's uploaded, you can't see where it's kept, who can open it, how long it stays, or whether it trains someone else's AI. Your pharmacy is still accountable for it.

How it works

Three steps. Zero uploads.

1

Pour and point

Spread one medication on a counting tray, keep labels out of the frame, and hold your phone above it.

2

Counted on your phone

Eir Count's AI estimates how many tablets or capsules are on the tray, on the phone itself. No upload, no server, no waiting on Wi-Fi.

3

Verify and dispense

Each pill Eir counted is marked with a dot, so you can check the count against the tray. Eir Count is a counting aid: you verify every count before dispensing.

Edge AI. Zero cloud.

What happens on the counter stays on the counter.

Eir Count runs its AI at the edge: on the phone in your hand, not in a data centre. Counting has no upload step to secure, so a label that slips into the frame stays in the pharmacy. Privacy isn't a setting you have to find. It's how Eir Count is built.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Eir Count send my photos anywhere?

Not to count them. Counting happens entirely on your phone. A photo leaves it only when you choose: when you share a count, export an audit report or email support, or when pharmacy sharing sends counts to your own pharmacy's devices over the store Wi-Fi, encrypted with your pharmacy's key.

Does the app send anything else?

A little, and never your photos or patient details. It checks your subscription with Apple or Google. On Android, the barcode scanner is a Google component (ML Kit) that sends Google technical usage data about the scanner, never your photos or barcodes. Eir Count has no ads, no tracking and no accounts.

What if a patient label ends up in the photo?

It isn't uploaded. Eir Count never sends photos anywhere to count them, so whatever is in the frame stays in your pharmacy. Still, keep prescription labels and anything that identifies a patient out of the frame, because a photo goes wherever you choose to share or export it. You also choose how long photos are kept on the phone, from two weeks to forever.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to count. The counting model runs on the device, so counting works without Wi-Fi or mobile data. The app checks your subscription with the App Store or Google Play whenever the phone is online; if a phone stays offline for several days, it will ask you to connect so counting can continue.

How much does Eir Count cost?

CAD $5 a month plus applicable taxes, with unlimited counts and no per-count AI credits. New subscribers get a 14-day free trial. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel, which you can do any time in your App Store or Google Play account.

Do you offer pharmacy or group pricing?

Yes. Pharmacy and group licences are priced per store, with discounts for groups. Email info@asgardpharma.ca and we'll put a quote together.

Does Eir Count replace pharmacist verification?

No. Eir Count is a counting aid. It marks each pill it counted so you can check the count against the tray, and the pharmacist verifies every count before dispensing.
